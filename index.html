Our Story

Khufu’s is a contemporary Egyptian restaurant located at the foot of the Great Pyramids in Giza, Egypt. Opened in 2022, it offers a unique dining experience with stunning views of the pyramids. Founded by Giovanni Bolandrini and led by Chef Mostafa Seif, Khufu’s combines traditional Egyptian flavors with modern culinary techniques. More than just a place to eat, it immerses guests in Egyptian culture and hospitality, creating memorable moments and delicious dishes for every visitor.
